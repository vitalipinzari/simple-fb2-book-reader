# 01 — The product: Lanternleaf 0.6.0

This is the overview every other spec serves: what the reader is, what it does on each platform, its defaults
and controls, where it keeps its data, and which spec owns each feature. The user-facing description is the
README (verbatim in `ai/files/05-docs-assets-tools.md`); the exact behaviour, numbers and texts are in the module
specs 03–11. Where this overview and a module spec differ, the module spec wins.

## 3. Features (and the spec that owns each)

### Reading
- **GPU text rendering** — every glyph is a textured quad; FreeType atlas with light hinting and 4 horizontal
  sub-pixel positions; HarfBuzz shaping (kerning, ligatures, combining stress marks) with per-character font
  fallback (specs 07, 08).
- **Layout** — Knuth–Plass "total fit" line breaking with greedy fallback and emergency splitting of unbreakable
  strings (URLs), Liang hyphenation with the TeX `hyph-utf8` patterns for American English and Russian, Russian
  typographic rules (a dash never starts a line, a dialogue dash stays with its first word), justified text with
  first-line indents, headings that never end a page (specs 05, 07).
- **Reflow with a stable position** — the reading position is a text position (block, byte offset), not a page
  number; zoom (Ctrl+wheel, Ctrl+Plus/Minus, pinch) and window resizes keep the line being read on screen (specs
  07, 09).
- **One page or an open book** — *1 page* / *2 pages* (B), *Automatic* = two pages on a wide window; two pages
  need ≈18 characters per line on each page, otherwise a toast says the screen is too narrow (specs 09, 10).
- **Page turns** — four styles in Settings → Page turning: 3D page curl (a mesh bent around a moving cylinder
  with lighting, a see-through back and a cast shadow; in book mode the right page flips over the spine), Slide,
  Fade out and in, and **Instant** (default: next/previous pages pre-rendered into textures in idle time)
  (specs 08, 09).
- **Page numbers** — counted in the background in time slices while the status bar shows (spec 07, 09).
- **Themes** — Day, Sepia, Dusk, Night amber (~600 nm), Night red (>610 nm); brightness slider (light themes dim
  the paper, dark themes the ink); optional clock-based day/night switch. Themes colour the text only: pictures
  keep their colours, web links are always blue and underlined (verbatim `Themes.cpp`; specs 09, 10).
- **Contents, footnotes, links** — table of contents panel; clicking a note number shows the note in a pop-up;
  web addresses (also written as plain text) open in the browser (specs 03, 04, 09, 10).
- **Find in the book** with highlighting; **Go to** a page or percentage (specs 09, 10).
- **Resume** — the last book reopens at the same place with the same window, font and theme; positions are kept
  per book with a content fingerprint, so a renamed or moved book still resumes (specs 04, 09; `Settings.cpp`).

### Books
- **FB2**: sections, titles, epigraphs, poems and stanzas, citations, subtitles, emphasis, strong,
  strikethrough, sup/sub, code, footnotes, cover and block pictures; tolerant of broken files; `.fb2.zip` opens
  directly (spec 04).
- **TXT**: paragraphs rebuilt from hard-wrapped, indented or blank-line separated text; chapter headings
  (Chapter, Глава, Часть, roman numerals …) go to the contents; `--` and spaced hyphens become em dashes;
  Project Gutenberg boilerplate and `_italic_` markup handled (spec 04).
- **Encodings**: UTF-8, UTF-16 LE/BE, Windows-1251, KOI8-R, CP866, ISO-8859-5 (and Windows-1252) detected by
  letter-frequency analysis; the FB2 XML declaration is honoured and a wrong one detected (spec 03).
- **Languages**: English and Russian fully (hyphenation); other Latin and Cyrillic languages render but are not
  hyphenated.

### Finding books (Ctrl+F)
Background search of chosen folders or all drives with streaming results: by **file name** (with
transliteration: *Толстой*, *Tolstoy* and *Tolstoj* all find `Tolstoy_Voyna-i-mir.fb2.zip`), by **title, author,
series** (FB2 metadata, cached), by **text inside books** (whole phrase, ignoring case, ё/е and typographic
quotes); an empty query lists every book found. System folders, licence/readme files, tiny `.txt` files and
OneDrive online-only placeholders are skipped (specs 06, 10; `Platform.cpp`).

### Shell
- **Toolbar** at the top and **status bar** at the bottom come and go together (pointer at the top or bottom
  edge; a tap in the middle of the page on phones; right click or F10 pins them). Buttons that do not fit go into
  **Menu** at the toolbar's right end, which always has *About*. While the bars show, the page is laid out
  between them; hidden, the text fills the screen (specs 09, 10).
- **Status bar**: chapter (opens the contents), progress bar with chapter marks (click or drag to jump), page
  number and percentage (click to type one in) (spec 10).
- **Panels** (Library: Recent / Find books / Find in book; Contents; Settings; Go to; Help; About; file browser)
  open one at a time in the top-left corner of the reading area and cover half of it (left half on a wide
  screen, top half on a tall one); a click or tap outside a menu or panel closes it and returns to the page
  (spec 10).
- **Desktop window**: cannot be made smaller than 10 % of its monitor's width and height; full screen with F11
  or Alt+Enter; drag and drop of books; file association friendly (`Lanternleaf.exe book.fb2`) (spec 09).
- **Idle CPU use is zero**: the loop sleeps until something happens (spec 09).
- **Android**: immersive full screen and keep-screen-on (both in Settings), safe area for notches and system
  bars, Back closes the open panel or leaves the app, *All files access* flow, "Open with Lanternleaf" for
  FB2/TXT/ZIP, in-app file browser, own screen brightness by swiping on the left part of the page (specs 09–11).
- **Automation**: screenshot mode renders off-screen and writes a PNG, used for visual checks (specs 09, 13).

## 4. Controls

Desktop (authoritative list with modifiers and edge cases: spec 09):

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Next / previous page | → ↓ PgDn Space, mouse wheel, click the right part of the page / ← ↑ PgUp Shift+Space Backspace, click the left third |
| First / last page | Home / End |
| Bigger / smaller text | Ctrl + wheel, Ctrl+Plus / Ctrl+Minus, pinch; Ctrl+0 resets |
| Open a book | Ctrl+O or drag a file onto the window |
| Find books on disk / in this book | Ctrl+F / Ctrl+Shift+F |
| Recent books | Ctrl+L |
| Contents, Go to, Settings, Help | T, Ctrl+G, Ctrl+Comma, F1 |
| Colour theme | C / Shift+C, Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+5 |
| One page / two pages | B |
| Toolbar and status bar | pointer at the top or bottom edge; right click or F10 pins them |
| Full screen | F11 or Alt+Enter |
| Close panel | Esc (closes the Menu first when it is open) |

Touch (spec 09 has the thresholds):

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Turn pages | tap the right part / left third of the page, or swipe |
| Bigger / smaller text | two-finger pinch (reflows, keeps the place) or A+ / A− |
| Menu and status bar | tap the middle of the page; any tap on the page outside them hides them again |
| Screen brightness | swipe up or down on the left part of the page |
| Panels | tap the page beside them, or Back, to return to reading; a vertical drag always scrolls |
| Back button | closes the open panel (or the Menu); on the page it leaves the app (the position is saved) |

## 5. Defaults (first start)

From the verbatim `src/app/Settings.h`: Day theme, one page (`columns = "1"`), instant page turns
(`pageTurn = "none"`, `turnSeconds = 0.5`), PT Serif, font size 21 logical px on desktop / 19 dp on Android,
line spacing 1.4, margins 6 % of the width, paragraph indent 1.5 em, no paragraph spacing, justified,
hyphenated, max line width 40 em in single-column mode, brightness 1.0, auto night off (21:00–07:00, Day ↔ Night
amber), window 1180 × 820 at the system default position, full screen off on desktop / on (immersive) on Android,
keep screen on, screen brightness −1 (follow the system), search by name and metadata (not content), skip system
folders and tiny TXT files, search roots = the platform defaults (Windows: Downloads, Documents, Desktop;
Android: Download, Documents, Books, Telegram folders that exist).

## 6. Data on disk

The per-user folder is `SDL_GetPrefPath("", "Lanternleaf")` (Windows: `%APPDATA%\Lanternleaf\`; Android: the
app's internal files folder) unless `--settings-dir <dir>` is given. It holds:

| File | Content | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `settings.json` | settings, recent books with positions and fingerprints, last query, search roots | verbatim `Settings.cpp` |
| `library-cache.json` | metadata cache of the library search (keyed by path, validated by size and mtime) | spec 06 |
| `imgui.ini` | Dear ImGui window state | spec 09 |
| `lanternleaf.log` | log of the session (start-up facts, errors, the instant-turn statistic on exit) | spec 09 |

Android extras: books handed over by other apps ("Open with") are copied to `<files>/opened/<name>`;
`getExternalFilesDir(null)` is created at start so automation can use `/sdcard/Android/data/app.lanternleaf.reader/files/`.

## 7. Command line

`Lanternleaf.exe [options] [book]` — the positional argument opens a book. Options (spec 09 is authoritative):
`--settings-dir <dir>`, `--screenshot <out.png>`, `--size <W>x<H>` (≥ 200 each, default 1280×820), `--frames <n>`,
`--goto <percent>`, `--next <pages>`, `--turn <t>` (freezes a curl/slide/fade at t), `--panel
toc|settings|help|about|library|goto|browser|folders|storage|toolbar`, `--search <query>`, `--search-root <dir>`
(repeatable), `--search-content`, `--find <phrase>`, `--find-go`, `--click <x>,<y>` (repeatable), `--allow-save`,
and the settings overrides `--theme <id>`, `--font-size <px>` (clamped 8…120), `--columns auto|1|2`,
`--page-turn curl|slide|fade|none`, `--font <family>`. On Android the same arguments come from the intent extra
`args` (`am start … --es args '…'`).

## 8. Known limitations (keep them)

- Right-to-left and vertical scripts are shaped but not laid out right to left.
- Pictures inside a running paragraph are skipped; block pictures and the cover are shown.
- FB2 tables are simplified to rows with `|` separators.
- No text selection, copying, bookmarks or annotations.
- No cloud sync (it existed briefly before 0.3.0 and was removed; do not add it).
